Connection & notification operations

Build real event flows—not pretend status lights.

01 · Event intake

AI task responses

Each AI engine needs a webhook, inbox connector, or scheduled status check. The hub can then log the task, response, timestamp and source.

02 · Delivery

Email or SMS alerts

Email can use a transactional email provider; SMS can use a messaging provider. Credentials stay server-side and delivery should be tested before being marked active.

03 · Trust & control

Approval and audit trail

Use authenticated endpoints, access rules, rate limits and event logs. Require approval before sending sensitive messages or triggering financial, account or robot actions.
